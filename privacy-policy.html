Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

📅 Effective Date: June 2026

Introduction

SSS Little Stars Primary School is committed to protecting the privacy of parents, guardians, and visitors who use this website. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect your name, phone number, child's age, and any message you provide. We do not collect any other personal data automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used only to respond to your enquiry about admissions or school-related questions. We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Form submissions are stored securely through Netlify Forms. We retain this data only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry.

4. Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All enquiries are submitted by parents or guardians only.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any tracking technologies. Your browsing on this site is completely private.
